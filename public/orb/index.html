      Good to know

      The practical bits

      Everything a manager usually asks, in one place.

      How long is a night?

      About two and a half hours. Doors at 7:00 PM, the first round at 7:30, winners at about 9:45. After that the room is yours and your DJ's.

      Which nights?

      Weeknights, Monday to Wednesday. We start with one trial night. If it works, we lock a regular slot.

      How does the quiz work?

      Teams of up to 6, five rounds and 65 questions: Emoji Pictionary (15), Name the Movie (15), Dialogue Decoder (10), Terribly Explained Plots (15) and the Jackpot Round (10). Each team gets one answer sheet and hands it in at the end of every round. The top score is 1,100.

      Do guests need to know Malayalam?

      They need to love Malayalam films. It's all Malayalam cinema, from old classics to recent releases. Spelling doesn't matter and Manglish counts, so nobody has to write in Malayalam script.

      What do you need from The Orb?

      A screen or TV for the questions, a mic and a speaker, and tables for the teams. A prize for the winners is a nice extra. We'll check the screen, mic and seating with you before the first night.

      What do you take care of?

      Everything else: the questions, slides, answer sheets and scoring, a live quizmaster on the mic, the posters and edited videos, the online listing and bookings, and setup and pack-down. You don't need to put any staff on it.

      How do guests book?

      We list the night online and handle the bookings. You don't take reservations for it.

      Can The Orb help with promotion?

      Yes, and it would help a lot, because we're just starting out. Share our posters and reels on your pages and screens, and send us clips and photos from your venue. We'll edit them into ads and reels for your night.

      What happens after the trial night?

      You see the crowd for yourself. If it works for The Orb, we lock a regular slot and agree on a simple revenue share.

      Who do I talk to?

      James J K, founder and quizmaster. Call or message +91 70853 88330, email itsolamco@gmail.com, or find us on Instagram at @itsolamco.
