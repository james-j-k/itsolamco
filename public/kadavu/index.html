Why Kadavu

A riverside full of teams

A reason to come on a weeknight

Groups of friends need a reason to go out midweek. A quiz with a winner is a reason, and a river view makes them stay.

Tables that order

Teams of up to 6 sit for two hours and share cocktails and starters. A winners’ cocktail round gets the whole room talking about your new menu.

Easy to reach

Eight minutes from Vadakkekota metro and right off the main road. That matters when we sell a night to ten teams from across Kochi.

People who book, show up

Teams register on itsolamco.in. We can add an entry fee that’s fully redeemable on food and drinks, so the people who book actually come.
